Privacy Policy

Effective October 2026

Clips4U is designed primarily as a local Windows video-processing tool. The Windows Store Edition processes user-selected video and audio on the user's device for its core clipping, transcription, captioning, and rendering workflow.

The current Clips4U Windows Store Edition does not require users to upload their videos to a Clips4U-operated server for core processing. Preferences, settings, diagnostic logs, and generated clips may be stored locally on the user's Windows device.

The Creator Crawler may connect to public third-party web services and websites to discover public video metadata and retrieve user-requested source material. Those third-party services may receive normal network information such as IP address, browser/client information, and request details under their own privacy policies.

Clips4U does not intentionally sell personal information. If future versions add accounts, cloud storage, analytics, advertising, or other network services, this policy may be updated before those features are offered.

Users are responsible for ensuring that they have the rights and permissions necessary for media they download, edit, save, or publish.
